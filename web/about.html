About CropXAI

A leaf-disease checker that shows its reasoning, built as a research project on explainable AI.

What it is

CropXAI recognises 38 leaf conditions across 14 crops from one photo, shows where the model looked and whether that explanation is honest, and suggests what to do. It is a research prototype built around one question: can a small model that runs on a phone still explain its decisions faithfully?

  • Runs entirely on your device. The model (ONNX Runtime Web, WebAssembly) and the heatmap run in the browser; no photo is uploaded. After the first visit it works offline and can be installed as an app.
  • Explains every answer. Grad-CAM++ heatmap, evidence for the 2nd and 3rd guesses, a live faithfulness test, and an exact breakdown of the score.
  • Honest about its limits. It says "Not sure" below a validated confidence threshold, and shows its measured field accuracy.

How it was built

  1. Data. PlantVillage lab photos (38 classes) for training; PlantDoc real-field photos kept aside as an untouched field test. Near-duplicates are removed with perceptual hashing, and a guard blocks field photos from training and tuning.
  2. Models. MobileNetV3-Large and EfficientNet-B0 (ImageNet pre-trained, frozen or fully fine-tuned) and a small CNN from scratch, each trained with 3 seeds.
  3. Compression. Structured channel pruning (50%, 70%) with and without fine-tuning, and INT8 quantization (XNNPACK scheme used on Android).
  4. Explanations. Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++ and Score-CAM, scored with deletion/insertion AUC, Average Drop, Increase in Confidence and ROAD, plus stability under compression.
  5. App. The trained model is exported to ONNX with two outputs (scores and feature maps), so the browser computes Grad-CAM++ in closed form from a single forward pass.
