    Tips for a good photo

    {{icon:check}} Do

    • Photograph one leaf, filling most of the frame.
    • Pick a leaf that shows the problem clearly, top side up.
    • Use daylight or shade, with the leaf in sharp focus.
    • If you can, hold it against a plain background (a sheet of paper or your palm).

    {{icon:alert}} Avoid

    • Whole plants, rows or fields, fruit, flowers or stems.
    • Blurry, very dark or very bright photos, harsh shadows.
    • Several overlapping leaves or lots of soil in view.
    • Crops that are not in the list: the model will still guess one of its classes.

    Why this matters: the model learned from lab photos of single leaves on plain backgrounds, and is much less accurate on busy field photos (see why).
