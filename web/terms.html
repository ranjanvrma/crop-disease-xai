Terms of use

CropXAI is a free research prototype. Please read these points before relying on anything it shows.

A research prototype, not professional advice

CropXAI is an experiment in explainable AI. It has not been tested with farmers and is not a certified diagnostic tool. Its diagnoses can be wrong, especially on real field photos, and its remedies are general guidance that has not been reviewed by a plant pathologist. Confirm any diagnosis with a local agronomist or agricultural extension service before acting on it.

Pesticides and treatments

Chemical options are listed as active ingredients, not products. Whether a product is permitted, and at what rate and timing, differs by country and crop. Always follow the product label and your local regulations.

Provided as-is

The site is provided free of charge and as-is, without any guarantee that it is accurate, complete or available. You use it at your own risk.

Acceptable use

Use the site for its purpose: checking leaf photos and reading about the method. Please don't try to disrupt it or the hosting service.

Data, code and credits

The source code is public on GitHub. The models were trained on the PlantVillage dataset and evaluated on the PlantDoc dataset; see sources and credits. Each dataset has its own licence and citation terms.

Your privacy

Photos are checked on your device and never uploaded. See the privacy notice.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the project develops; the date at the top shows the latest version.
