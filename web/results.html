Table 2 · Explanation quality

Faithfulness of each heatmap method (↓ lower is better, ↑ higher is better), on 300 lab test images and 300 field images (Score-CAM on 50). Pointing game and IoU need lesion masks, which do not exist yet, so they show "—". See Explainability for what each metric means.
