Privacy notice

In short: your photos are checked inside your browser and never leave your device. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or ads.

Your photos

When you take or choose a photo, the model, the heatmap and every check run on your device, inside the browser (ONNX Runtime Web and WebAssembly). The photo is not uploaded, not sent to any server, and not kept after you leave or reload the page.

What is stored on your device

  • The app and the model files, kept in your browser's offline cache (a service worker) so the site works without a connection.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark), if you change it, saved in your browser's local storage under cropxai-theme.

Neither contains information about you or your photos. A chosen model can appear in the page address (?model=…); it is not stored anywhere else.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no forms that send data.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or third-party scripts. Fonts and icons are served from this site, not from third parties.

Hosting

The site is a set of static files served by Render. Like any web host, Render's servers receive the standard information your browser sends with every request (such as your IP address, browser type and the files requested) in order to deliver the pages. The project does not combine that information with anything else.

Removing stored data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings (often called "Site settings" or "Clear browsing data"), or uninstall the app if you installed it. That removes the offline cache and the theme choice.

Questions and changes

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub repository. If this notice changes, the date at the top will change with it.
